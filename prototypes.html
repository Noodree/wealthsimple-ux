Leave accounts out of net worth

“When I look at my net worth, I want to ignore my kids’ RESPs. That money isn’t mine, so I don’t want it in the total.” Five ways to solve it, each one a working prototype.

A

Toggle in the account panel

An “Include in net worth” switch sits with each account’s details. Left-out accounts stay in the list, greyed out.

Try it: open Alex’s RESP and turn the switch off.

Open prototype A
B
My lean

Customize the total

A control next to the total opens a checklist of every account, with a live preview. A chip shows the total is filtered.

Try it: click the sliders next to the total and untick both RESPs.

Open prototype B
C

Not counted section

Left-out accounts move to a dashed “Not counted” section at the bottom of the list, so the reason for a lower total is always visible.

Try it: use the ⋯ on an RESP and choose Move to Not counted.

Open prototype C
D

Quick hide on the row

An eye icon on hover (desktop) or a swipe (mobile) removes an account in one move, with an undo toast.

Try it: hover an RESP and click the eye. Narrow the window and swipe a row left.

Open prototype D
E

Saved views

Name a set of accounts as a view, like “My money”, and switch between it and All accounts. Chart, bar and list follow the view.

Try it: click + New view, untick the RESPs, name it and save.

Open prototype E
